The program
What exactly is Squad Institute?
Work experience on startup squads — for grads, post-grads, and career switchers. You apply for an open spot, get a buddy on your squad, and complete an 8-week Sprint with two reviewed deliverables and a portfolio. An optional 52-week path goes deeper. It’s not a course, and it’s not fake busywork.
What about career coaching if I’m out of a job?
Career coaching is for people between jobs — when rent, visa, or search burnout needs attention before an 8-week Sprint. We reply within five business days. You can apply for a squad spot when you’re ready.
Is this a job? Am I an employee?
You apply like it’s a job — that’s the energy. The 8-week Sprint is mentored work experience on a startup squad, not a wage employment contract by default. The optional 52-week path has separate written terms. We won’t pretend you’re on payroll if you’re not.
What’s the Sprint vs the year path?
8-week Sprint is the starting program: join a squad, do real product work, complete two deliverables, and leave with a portfolio and participation record. The 52-week path is optional — deeper experience and optionally startup ownership under a separate agreement.
What will I actually do?
You do track work — Marketing, Engineering, Business Analyst, Product Owner, UX, Sales, or Data — on a repeatable lifecycle: scope, map, plan, build, review, ship. Tools appear when the work needs them. You leave with artifacts and a show-up record, not a certificate alone.
Do I learn to use AI?
Yes — because the market now expects it. Recruiters are drowning in generic AI applications; what employers want is someone who can harness AI for their specific role and industry — draft with it, review it, ship work they still own. On every pathway you practice that in Instrument and Build: AI assists, humans review to a rubric, you keep the artifact. See pathways for role-specific examples.
What is the participation record?
A timeline recruiters can open showing when you were active or paused. Hard weeks don’t count against you if you tell us. Going quiet on the work is visible — and triggers a human check-in. We review the work, not your worth.
Money & equity
What does it cost?
Applying and the 8-week Sprint aren’t sold as a day-one fee. Recommended tools are yours to clear when work needs them. The 52-week path may use a separate buy-now-pay-later or income-share agreement — capped, threshold-based, and in writing. You review numbers before you convert, not before you apply. Worth knowing: take independent advice before signing any credit/partnership agreement. Cooling-off and deferment rules are in that document.
When and how do I actually pay?
You pay nothing until your income passes the agreed threshold. Below it, payments are deferred — paused, with no interest building up. Once you're earning above the threshold, contributions begin as a share of income, up to the fixed cap and never beyond it. The mechanics are spelled out in your agreement.
How does the equity work?
As a founding member you take a stake in the venture, vesting over time. You're not doing free work for someone else — you're building something you partly own. The vesting schedule and full terms are set out in your agreement, so you know exactly what you hold and when.
What happens if I leave early?
You're free to leave — these are voluntary ventures. What you keep, how vesting is treated, and any remaining obligations are all defined in your agreement. We'd genuinely rather you read that section carefully (and take independent advice) before signing than gloss over it here, because the specifics matter.
Eligibility
Is the program online?
Yes — 100% online. Squads, buddy check-ins, reviews, and shipping all run remotely. You need a reliable internet connection and the tools your track clears — not a city campus seat.
Who can apply?
Graduates, post-graduates, and career switchers who can commit Sprint hours and are eligible to work or train in Australia. Pathways include Marketing, Engineering, Business Analyst, Product Owner, UX, Sales, and Data. The program itself is 100% online.
Can I apply if I'm on a student or work visa?
Possibly — it depends on your visa’s work and study conditions, which differ a lot by type. Start an application and we’ll walk through eligibility with you early.
Do I need to be an experienced developer already?
No — the whole point is to take you from "not enough experience" to genuinely hireable. You'll need some foundation to contribute to a real build, and the application helps us understand where you're at and which squad fits. We'd rather place you well than take everyone.
After the program
Do you guarantee me a job at the end?
No — and anyone who promises that is selling you something. What we give you is the thing most grads are missing: genuine, recent, shippable experience and people who can vouch for your work. That's what moves you up the pile in a real hiring process. The applying, interviewing and landing is still yours to do — and our mentors help you do it.
What do I walk away with?
At minimum after the Sprint: two reviewed deliverables, squad references, a portfolio, and a participation record. On the 52-week path you get deeper experience and — if you choose it — startup ownership under a separate agreement. No honest program guarantees a job.
