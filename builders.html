Maya R.
Kelpie · backend · international

Her visa clock is running and family already paid for the move. She needed to leave with something she could open — not another empty month.

2 deliverables
01 · route constraint sketch
02 · offline fallback
01
Took the seat. Buddy matched same day. Cleared tools. Pocket: if nothing lands, the visa story gets ugly.
02
Deliverable 1 — route constraint sketch, reviewed. First thing she could show. deliverable
03
Rough stretch — rent + job apps. Paused two days, didn’t ghost. Then Deliverable 2 — offline fallback routing. deliverable
04
Pack + showcase. Converted to the 52-week path — proof before the clock, not after.
AK
Aisha K.
Tollgate · full-stack · working while studying

Dad drives trucks. Mum cleans. She’s covered rent since second year — unpaid internships were never on the table. Core fear: one wrong bet and she falls back.

2 deliverables
01 · quote → invoice wire
02 · GST line items
01
Applied from the break room after closing. Buddy scoped a task she could finish between shifts — no guilt for saying no to extra hours.
02
Deliverable 1 — quote → invoice wireframe, reviewed. First portfolio piece that wasn’t a uni assignment. Sent the link to her mum; she didn’t get it but knew it was real. deliverable
03
Landlord raised rent. Almost picked up a sixth shift. Buddy shrank Deliverable 2 to GST line items only. Still finished. deliverable
04
Portfolio review. Two artifacts + squad reference. Referred two people from retail who asked how she “got into tech.”
LT
Liam T.
Sandbar · product · career change

Fourteen years in venues. Mortgage, two kids, parents asking when he’ll “get a real job again.” Sunk cost on his mind — needed proof the switch wasn’t a midlife whim before savings ran out.

2 deliverables
01 · member renew flow
02 · waitlist v1
01
Application said career change out loud. Joined Sandbar; buddy treated his roster ops background as product signal, not a gap.
02
Deliverable 1 — member renew flow. Review pushed acceptance criteria; he reworked once without reading it as “too old to learn.” deliverable
03
Compared himself to 22-year-olds on LinkedIn. Reviewer stayed on the work. Deliverable 2 — waitlist v1. Kids asleep, finished at 11pm. deliverable
04
Portfolio review. The switch has receipts now — not just a cover letter story.
NP
Noah P.
Ledger · QA / ops · first in family

First in his family through uni. Dad proud but quiet; mum asks when he’ll get “a proper job.” No uncle in tech, no map for interviews or portfolios — achievement guilt on both sides.

2 deliverables
01 · bug bash notes
02 · regression pack

Didn’t know what “scope” meant. Buddy walked the first task line by line — no jargon gatekeeping.

03

04
Portfolio review. Two things she can open in an interview. Parents finally have something concrete to point at.
