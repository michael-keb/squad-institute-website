Candidate funnel · Video script

The 90-second straight version.

The script sent to people who responded to the SEEK listing. It says what the program is, what it isn't, who it's for, and what happens next — then gets out of the way.

~285 words · 105–115 sec at 150 wpm Tone · calm, adult, transparent Format · talking head or voiceover
Working document. This is the recording script, not a public page — don't link it from the site nav. Read it aloud before recording; the pause marks are part of the structure.
01 · Anchor — the SEEK listing

Hi — I'm [name] from Squad Institute.

You're watching this because you responded to our listing on SEEK. Before you spend any more of your time, I want to be straight with you about what this is, and what it isn't.

[ pause ]
02 · What this is

Squad Institute is a program where early-career engineers join a small squad and build a real startup together, over six to twelve months, mentored by senior engineers. You join as a founding member. You take an equity stake in what you build. It costs nothing upfront — you contribute later as a capped share of your income, and only once you're earning above a threshold. All of that sits in a written agreement you read before you commit, with a cooling-off period after signing.

[ pause ]
03 · What this is not

Now the other half.

This is not a job. There's no salary, and you won't be an employee. It's not a placement service. And nobody hands you instructions — you and your squad own the work and the decisions. We also don't guarantee you a job at the end. No honest program can. What you finish with is shipped software, references from people who watched you build it, and your stake in the venture.

[ pause ]
04 · Who it's for — and not for

It's for people early in their career who are missing real experience, and are willing to own something for six to twelve months to get it. It's not for you if you need an income now, or you want a course with a syllabus.

[ pause ]
05 · What happens next

If you're interested, here's what happens next. A short form — about fifteen minutes. Then a short video going deeper than this one. Then a call, where we check fit and eligibility in both directions — including visa conditions, if that applies to you. Nothing is binding until you've read and signed the agreement.

06 · Opt-out

And if this isn't for you — no problem. Just ignore this. There's no follow-up and no pressure.

Thanks for your time either way.

End · ~110 sec

Read-aloud notes

  • Target pace is conversational, not presenter-speed. If it comes in under 90 seconds, you're rushing the pauses.
  • The four pause marks are structural — they separate what it is, what it's not, who it's for, and what's next. Hold each for a beat.
  • Don't smile-read the "not a job" section. Flat and factual is what makes it land as trustworthy.
  • Cutting to 90 seconds, in order: the visa-conditions clause first, then the cooling-off clause. Never cut the not-a-job paragraph or the opt-out line.
