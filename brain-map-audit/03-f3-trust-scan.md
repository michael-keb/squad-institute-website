# Round 3 — F3 Trust scan

**Map node:** *Does the source feel like the ones that ghosted or exploited me?*  
**Pages:** all marketing pages, especially `active-squads.html`, `builders.html`, `faq.html`, disclaimers  
**Engine links:** E11 institutional distrust, S2 ghosting, S6 wage theft

---

## Objections

1. **Live / recruiting theater.** Active squads + pulsing “Recruiting” pills + fake-feeling member counts risk *startup fake social proof* — same smell as hustle funnels that ghost after payment.
2. **Illustrative builders vs claimed reality.** Personas (Maya, Aisha…) read as samples; if not labeled “illustrative / composite,” distrust spikes when someone asks for LinkedIn and finds nothing.
3. **ISA / equity / BNPL in FAQ** next to “no job guarantee” — classic exploitation pattern memory for intl + working-class grads. Even if fair, the *shape* triggers E11.
4. **“Seats open” + urgency** without named humans, ABN, or named mentors on home → opaque institution.
5. **Apply form that goes nowhere visible.** If submit is local/demo with no human reply SLA, confirms ghosting expectation.
6. **Product language leak.** Pocket, L2, lifecycle jargon on builders/how-it-works can feel like internal cult docs, not peer voice.
7. **Contact exists but isn’t hero-adjacent.** Trust often needs “talk to a human before apply” — currently secondary.

---

## Delight / come-back-again

1. **Label samples honestly:** “Illustrative Sprint arcs · real ships linked when live” — integrity > vanity (matches graph doctrine).
2. **Show the anti-ghost:** “We reply in X days or your seat hold auto-releases” on apply success + FAQ.
3. **Named trust objects:** One real mentor face/name, one real squad repo/demo link, one real (redacted) graph screenshot — even one of each beats six fake cards.
4. **Dull money:** Keep FAQ pin about independent advice; add a one-page “What we never do” (no fake jobs, no wage theft, no charging for apply).
5. **Peer-shaped voice:** Promote builders’ private pocket lines and rough weeks — trust via vulnerability, not polish.
6. **Return hook:** Weekly “squad truth” email: *what shipped, what paused, open seats* — consistent, dull, checkable. Distrust drops when the feed doesn’t lie.

---

## Round verdict

Builders + FAQ honesty **help F3**. Active squads / live pulses **hurt F3** if data isn’t real. Money path **needs boring transparency** or ST1/P1 bounce hard.

**Highest-leverage fix:** Truth labels + reply SLA + one verifiable artifact link on Active Squads.
