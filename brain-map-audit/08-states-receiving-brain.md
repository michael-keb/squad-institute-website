## Objections by state

### ST1 Survival mode
- Site asks for attention (story, graph, ideology) before survival answers.
- Long builders / how-it-works = invisible.
- Objection: “Cool story — does this pay / keep visa / get a hire this month?”

### ST2 Search burnout
- Pain copy (“ghosted,” “void”) can *resonate then exhaust* if next step is a long essay or form.
- Multiple CTAs = decision fatigue → avoidance (E8).
- Objection: “I can’t do another program funnel. Give me one tap.”

### ST3 Normative stress (anxious but functioning)
- Best current fit for home voice — but still risks identity threat on “red/absent.”
- May SKIM FAQ money and get spooked later (conversion leak).
- Objection: “Is the graph going to punish me when I’m already anxious?”

### ST4 Stable + exploring
- Will enjoy builders and active squads; may distrust illustrative content (F3).
- May over-index on Venture/equity and miss Sprint door.
- Objection: “Show me real repos or I’m out.”

**Highest-leverage fix:** ST1/ST2 door variants; don’t make everyone eat the ST4 novel first.
