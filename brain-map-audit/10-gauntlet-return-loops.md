## Objections that prevent coming back

1. **One-shot pitch fatigue** — if the whole doctrine is spent on first visit, no reason to return.
2. **No living feed** — static HTML with “live” cues that don’t update = trust decay on revisit.
3. **Apply black hole** — submit without human rhythm = confirmed ghosting.
4. **Comparison hangover** — remembered only the Venture convert, not the pause.
5. **Unresolved money fear** — bookmark FAQ, never reopen.
6. **No personal stake remembered** — site doesn’t echo *their* gate (visa/shifts/map).

## Final round verdict

The website **already speaks the brain map’s language** (structure over deficit, ships, pause, personas on builders). It **fails the map’s order of operations**: survival and agency at the door, trust that isn’t theater, bandwidth for taxed brains, then comparison-safe proof.

**Win condition:** a stranger in ST1/ST2 can answer in 60 seconds — *what I get soon, what it costs, one move, and that you won’t ghost me* — then return for peer ships and a human, not another brochure.
