# Round 1 — F1 Survival relevance

**Map node:** *Does this help me eat, pay rent, keep my visa, get hired soon?*  
**Pages:** `index.html`, `faq.html`, `apply.html`, `builders.html`, footer disclaimers  
**Engine links:** E4 scarcity, E9 guilt/family, E10 settle vs hold-out

---

## Objections

1. **“Work experience ≠ rent.”** Hero sells squads and a graph; ST1 brains ask for hours, pay, and time-to-hire. “Not a wage by default” (FAQ) lands as *unpaid filter* (S4) unless reframed as *faster hire proof*.
2. **Visa clock not in the first viewport.** Visa check lives in FAQ/apply sidebar — too late for Aarav-type F1. Home meta is Online / 8 wk / 52 wk / tracks — not *visa-safe pathway*.
3. **Cost fog.** FAQ: Sprint “not sold as day-one finance,” then BNPL/ISA language for year path. Survival brains hear *trap later* before they hear *free enough to try*.
4. **No “soon” number.** “Get hired soon” has no weeks-to-first-artifact, first-demo, or pack-ready date on home. 8 weeks feels long when rent is due Friday.
5. **Tool cost buried.** “Recommended tools are yours to clear” (FAQ) can fail F1 for Chloe — another bill before proof.
6. **Disclaimer undercuts the pitch.** Footer: no guarantee of employment/income. Honest for F3, but F1 reads it as *then why am I here?* if hero didn’t already answer *what I walk with this month*.

---

## Delight / come-back-again

1. **First-viewport survival line:** “First openable artifact in ~3 weeks · pack by week 7 · online so shifts/visa work hours still fit.”
2. **Money clarity strip:** Apply + Sprint: $0 program fee · tools optional until work needs them · year path numbers only after convert — pin this above the fold on apply + home.
3. **Visa micro-gate:** “Student/work visa? 2-min eligibility check before you invest hope” → contact/apply step that *reduces* risk, not adds form load.
4. **Builders as proof of soon:** Lead with W3 ship dates (“first thing she could show”) in home hero, not only on builders page.
5. **Return hook:** Email/SMS after apply: “Your seat hold + what happens in week 1 if rent/visa is tight” — F1 content that brings them back when scarcity spikes.
6. **Settle-vs-hold framing that helps Chloe:** Explicit “survival job OK; Sprint WIP=1 around shifts” on home + builders (Aisha story already has it — promote it).
