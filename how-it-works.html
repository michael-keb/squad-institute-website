Phase 01 — Apply

Pick a squad and tell us your situation.

01

Submit one application

Choose a pathway (Marketing, Engineering, Business Analyst, Product Owner, UX, Sales, or Data). Tell us your hours, visa status, and which squad interests you. Takes about 15 minutes.

7 pathways~15 min
Migrant or on a visa? Employers ask for Australian work experience — that’s what the Sprint is for. We’ll talk through eligibility early. If it’s not a fit, we say so straight — not after you’ve invested hope.
02

Get matched to a squad

We reply within five business days. You’re assigned a buddy — your main contact on the squad — who welcomes you and scopes your first task.

Phase 02 — 8-week Sprint

Do real product work for eight weeks.

03

Work through a real product lifecycle

You scope, plan, build, review, and deliver — the same steps a startup team uses. Tools are introduced when the work needs them. You also learn to use AI for your specific role, with human review on everything you submit.

Real SDLCAI for your role
04

Complete two deliverables

By the end you have two reviewed pieces of work, a portfolio recruiters can open, and a participation record showing when you were active or paused. Hard weeks don’t count against you if you tell us.

PortfolioParticipation record

Leave with proof — or stay longer if you want.

05

Exit with your portfolio

Finish the Sprint with deliverables, squad references, and a participation record. No pressure to continue. Many people leave here and job-hunt with stronger proof.
