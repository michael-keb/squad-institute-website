Gary Lau

Sydney · tech, finance, data & AI

Twenty years in corporate finance, then a pivot of his own. Gary holds the between-jobs brain — burnout, redirection, and leadership — without treating you like a résumé to fix.

Most coaching jumps straight to the résumé and negotiation script. Gary starts with who you actually are, what’s holding you back, and what you want work to look like — then builds the practical next move. Four-week programs for clarity and momentum, not a to-do list that fades in a fortnight.
