Welcome to the marketplace — not a course

What Squad Institute is, what it isn’t, and why we built a marketplace of startups for work experience instead of another certificate factory.

Squad Institute is a marketplace of startups for work experience. You claim a seat on a real squad, ship real work, and leave with proof recruiters can open — an engagement graph, artifacts, and people who can vouch for you.

It is not a course. There is no certificate-as-product. The program is the work: scope, map, plan, build, review, ship — on a live build with a crew that expects you to show up.

Why a marketplace

Graduates, post-grads, and career switchers keep hitting the same wall: experience required to get experience. We wanted a front door that feels like being chosen onto a team, not enrolled into content.

Startups on the board need people. You need a seat that ships. The marketplace is where those two needs meet.

What happens when you apply

One application — job-application energy. Soft seat held. A buddy says hello. A human replies within five business days. Rough week? We pause you; we don’t drop you.

If you’re ready, claim a seat. If you want to see squads first, browse the active board.
