Seven pathways, one squad lifecycle

Marketing, Engineering, Business Analyst, Product Owner, UX, Sales, and Data — same scope-to-ship engine, different artifacts you leave with.

Every pathway at Squad Institute runs the same squad lifecycle: orient, scope, model, plan, instrument, build, review, ship, reflect, expose. What changes is the craft you own and the artifacts you produce.

Pick your seat

  • Marketing — campaigns, content, funnels that ship with the product
  • Engineering — code on a live build, reviewed like a real PR
  • Business Analyst — requirements, process maps, edge cases before build
  • Product Owner — backlog, acceptance, ship decisions
  • UX / Design — flows and prototypes that leave Figma
  • Sales — outreach, discovery, CRM proof tied to a real product
  • Data — metrics, dashboards, definitions the squad can use

Not sure yet? That’s fine — tell us where you’re headed and we’ll match you to a seat or coaching first.
